RBX Toolkit

Manage your Roblox friends and grow your lives on TikTok, Twitch, YouTube and Kick: mass unfriend, requests in bulk, auto-accept, chat commands, a "Play with me" queue and an OBS widget.

👥 FriendsClean up your friend list in a click, with filters, protected friends and backups.
✉️ RequestsAccept or decline in bulk and spot bots by account age.
🔴 Live chatViewers type !add or !join in chat; the extension does the rest. TikTok is free, Twitch, YouTube and Kick are in Pro.
🎬 OBS widgetFriend counter and animated alerts, in 10 languages.
⭐ Pro: new computer or browser?Install the extension, open RBX Toolkit Pro, click I already paid and sign in with the email you paid with. Your subscription is tied to that email, not to the computer. Cancel anytime with Manage subscription.
