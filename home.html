RBX Toolkit

Manage your Roblox friends and grow your TikTok lives: mass unfriend, requests in bulk, auto-accept, chat commands, a "Play with me" queue and an OBS widget.

👥 FriendsClean up your friend list in a click, with filters, protected friends and backups.
✉️ RequestsAccept or decline in bulk and spot bots by account age.
🔴 TikTok liveViewers type !add or !join in chat; the extension does the rest.
🎬 OBS widgetFriend counter and animated alerts, in 10 languages.

Available in English, Español, Português, Français, Deutsch, Italiano, Polski, Türkçe, Русский and Bahasa Indonesia.
